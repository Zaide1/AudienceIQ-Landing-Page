Current issue:
coverage.percent is always 7 and untapped.percent is always 93, regardless of product, category, region, or generated segments. This makes the audience map feel fake.

Goal:
Make coverage and untapped values directional but dynamic.

Rules:
1. coverage.percent should vary based on the generated audience map.
2. coverage.percent must be clamped between 3 and 28.
3. untapped.percent must always equal 100 - coverage.percent.
4. coverage.people must equal reachableAudience.min * coverage.percent / 100, rounded.
5. untapped.min must equal reachableAudience.min - coverage.people, rounded.
6. untapped.max must equal reachableAudience.max * untapped.percent / 100, rounded.
7. Values should be honest MVP directional estimates, not official statistics.

Create a shared helper:
calculateCoverageEstimate(input)

It should consider:
- category
- region
- product specificity
- targetUsers specificity
- problem specificity
- whether evidenceSummary.sourceMode is "live_research" or "ai_hypothesis"
- segment concentration / whether one segment dominates
- whether the product sounds consumer, B2B, SaaS, technical, local, or niche

Suggested deterministic ranges:
- vague/general product: 3–6%
- broad consumer product: 5–9%
- normal startup/product idea: 7–13%
- niche B2B/SaaS/productivity tool: 12–20%
- highly specific technical product with live research signals: 18–28%

Clamp final coverage between 3 and 28.
