Clarification for the Audience Universe dot map:

This is not meant to be a mathematically exact dot chart.

It should be a beautiful visual audience landscape.

Meaning:
- Grey dots represent the whole reachable audience universe.
- Coloured dot clusters represent audience segments.
- The clusters should look like attractive grouped islands inside the grey dot field.
- The tooltip is just a static example of what hovering/clicking a segment would show.
- No actual hover interaction is needed for the landing page preview.
