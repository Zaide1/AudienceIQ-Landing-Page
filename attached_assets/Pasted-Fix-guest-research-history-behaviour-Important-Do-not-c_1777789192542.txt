Current issue:
Signed-out guest users can accumulate and view multiple research history sessions in localStorage. This makes a guest user look like they have account-level saved history and causes old product ideas to appear when reopening the site and going through the main CTA.

Desired behaviour:
Guest users should only have one active local research session. Full research history should only be available to signed-in users.

Rules:
1. If the user is signed out:
   - Do not show multiple saved research sessions in the Research history drawer.
   - The history button/drawer should either be hidden, disabled, or show the existing sign-up prompt: “Sign up to save and revisit research history.”
   - Guest users should not see old guest sessions listed as history.

2. Guest active session:
   - Keep one active guest research session for convenience.
   - When a guest starts a new research from the landing CTA/onboarding, replace the previous guest session instead of appending another session to history.
   - Clear or overwrite old guest research session records when a new guest research is created.
   - Preserve the current active guest map/chat only until they start a new guest research or clear it.

3. Signed-in users:
   - Continue to save every research session to Supabase.
   - Continue to show the Research history drawer normally.
   - Do not delete signed-in user history.
   - Do not mix guest local history with signed-in Supabase history after migration.

4. Migration:
   - If a guest signs up/signs in, migrate only the current active guest research session, not every stale local guest session.
   - After successful migration, clear local guest session storage so it cannot bleed into future guest or user sessions.

5. Storage cleanup:
   - For signed-out users, remove stale multi-session localStorage entries such as audense-research-sessions when starting a new guest research.
   - Keep only the active compatibility keys needed for the current guest session, such as the current map/chat/onboarding data.
   - Do not clear unrelated UI preferences.

Validation:
- Signed-out guest opens landing → clicks main CTA → completes onboarding → one current map exists.
- Same guest returns to landing → clicks main CTA again → new research replaces previous guest session instead of adding to history.
- Signed-out guest cannot open a list of 3+ old research sessions.
- Signed-out guest history button shows a sign-up prompt or is hidden.
- Signed-in user can create multiple research sessions and see them in history.
- Signing in migrates only the current active guest session.
- Signing out clears user-specific local cache and does not show previous account history to the next guest.
